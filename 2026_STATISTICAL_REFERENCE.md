# The 2026 PLL Statistical Reference

**Phase 8 — the definitive 2026 statistical layer.**

This is the top-level entry point to every published 2026 statistic. It states
what exists, what each artifact is for, which numbers may be led with, which
must travel with a caveat, and which do not exist at all. It supersedes nothing:
Phases 1–7 keep their own outputs, byte-identical, and Phase 8 consolidates
rather than re-derives.

Everything below is generated by `scripts/pll_build_phase8_stats.py`, verified
by `scripts/pll_validate_phase8.py` (24/24 checks pass) and `tests/test_phase8.py`
(70 tests), and audited by hand in
[`docs/2026_METRIC_SANITY_AUDIT.md`](docs/2026_METRIC_SANITY_AUDIT.md).

## 3. Publication status — the six-way split

Every metric in the catalog carries exactly one status. **A metric is not CORE
because it computes.**

| Status | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **CORE** | 126 | Lead with it. Well-defined denominator, reconciled to source, sample adequate for the claim it makes. |
| **CONTEXTUAL** | 74 | Publishable *alongside* something else — a denominator, a sample size, a companion metric, a stated caveat. Most rate metrics at 2026 sample sizes land here. |
| **EXPERIMENTAL** | 3 | Defensible construction, unvalidated interpretation. Publish with the construction visible; do not build on it. |
| **DIAGNOSTIC** | 16 | Exists to expose a disagreement, a coverage gap or a model assumption. Never a statistic about a team or a player. |
| **DEFERRED** | 6 | Wanted, not currently supportable; the missing input is named. |
| **UNSUPPORTED** | 8 | Cannot be built from this feed, and would mislead if faked. |

Only CORE, CONTEXTUAL and EXPERIMENTAL metrics appear as published columns or on
a leaderboard; validation check 18 fails if a DEFERRED or UNSUPPORTED concept
leaks into either.

## 4. Freeze classification — carrying 2026 into 2022–2025

| Class | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **FREEZE** | 192 | The definition applies unchanged to earlier seasons. |
| **FREEZE_WITH_CAVEAT** | 24 | The definition is stable; a stated property must travel with it into every season. |
| **REVISE_BEFORE_HISTORICAL** | 4 | Works for 2026; a multi-season pool changes what the right definition is. |
| **DO_NOT_USE** | 13 | Do not carry backward; do not publish. |

The four **REVISE_BEFORE_HISTORICAL** metrics are the whole usage-expectation
family (`expected_EPA_given_usage`, `EPA_vs_usage_expectation`,
`EPA_vs_usage_expectation_z`) plus the deferred `opponent_adjusted_value`. The
usage family is fitted on one season of 187 players and 2026's cross-validation
selected a **constant** model; a multi-season fit is the first thing that could
change it. Everything else in the system can be pointed at 2022–2025 as-is.

## 5. The seven facts that govern how these numbers may be read

1. **Raw and shrunk answer different questions and are never substituted.**
   Raw = what happened. Shrunk = the best estimate of ability. Both are
   published, side by side, on the same leaderboard row. Reordering between
   them is large: 189 of 192 shooters move, Spearman 0.675, maximum move 91
   places.
2. **A rate without its denominator is not a statistic.** Every leaderboard row
   carries one. Three players led shooting percentage at 1.000 — on 1, 2 and 1
   shots.
3. **Individual two-point shooting ability is not identifiable from 2026.** The
   observed between-player variance (0.0234) is *smaller* than binomial noise
   alone predicts (0.0276). Two-point production is published; two-point
   *ability* is not ranked, and the QUALIFIED scope for it is deliberately empty.
4. **Defensive value is PARTIAL, by construction.** It measures caused
   turnovers above the position group's per-game average and nothing else. A
   value of 0.0 does **not** mean "an average defender".
5. **Value totals are not comparable across positions.** The observed spread of
   `EPA_points_raw` differs by 6.6× between roles (goalie sd 9.59, defensive
   field 1.46). Every leaderboard row carries `canonical_position` and a
   `position_rank`; the overall rank on a value total is a record, not a ranking.
6. **Offensive play share is recorded opportunity share, not possession
   participation.** The feed has no lineup, substitution, shift or minutes data
   of any kind.
7. **About 19% of league turnovers are attributed to no player.** Player
   turnover sums give 1,369 against an official team total of 1,699. Every
   player-level turnover count and every usage measure built on turnovers is
   understated by an unknown, non-uniform amount.

## 6. Leaderboard qualification

Every gate is empirical-Bayes reliability ≥ 0.5 — the point at which the
posterior places more weight on the player's own record than on the league
prior. **No threshold is a round number chosen for convenience**; each rate's
prior strength was estimated from the data and implies its own trial count.

| Rate | Prior strength κ | Trials for reliability 0.5 | Players clearing it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Faceoff win % | 15.9 | 15.9 draws | 18 of 47 |
| Shooting % | 70.8 | 70.8 shots | 13 of 192 |
| One-point conversion % | 75.3 | 75.3 attempts | 8 of 168 |
| Turnovers per touch | 108.8 | 108.8 touches | 81 of 228 |
| Save % | 300.3 | 300.3 trials (saves + goals allowed) | **1 of 16** |
| Two-point conversion % | capped at 1e6 | unreachable | **0 of 127** |

The turnovers-per-touch prior is the one quantity Phase 8 estimated itself; it
uses Phase 6's own `beta_prior_by_moments`, imported rather than reimplemented.

The one-goalie and zero-shooter outcomes are **findings, not defects**: 2026
alone cannot separate goalies on save percentage, and cannot separate anyone on
two-point conversion. Both are reported as class-D sanity flags.

## 8. What Phase 8 deliberately did not build

No Statistical Tewaaraton. No MVP model. No overall award score. No composite
of any kind. No WAR, no replacement level, no cross-position weighting.

The catalog names the composite explicitly, once, as **UNSUPPORTED /
DO_NOT_USE**, with the argument for why: the unit is shared, the scale is not.
Validation check 19 scans five published surfaces for the forbidden patterns and
`tests/test_phase8.py` fails if one ever appears.

The full list of rejected and deferred concepts, each with the missing input
named, is in [`docs/2026_UNSUPPORTED_METRICS.md`](docs/2026_UNSUPPORTED_METRICS.md).

## 9. Reproducing everything

```
python3 scripts/pll_build_phase8_stats.py      # build
python3 scripts/pll_validate_phase8.py         # 24 checks
python3 -m unittest tests.test_phase8          # 70 tests
```

The build is deterministic: every query carries an explicit `ORDER BY`, the one
estimated quantity is a closed-form method-of-moments fit with no sampling, and
validation check 24 rebuilds every output into a scratch directory and compares
SHA-256.

To confirm nothing upstream moved:

```
python3 scripts/pll_validate_team_metrics.py            # 20/20
python3 scripts/pll_validate_player_value.py            # 24/24
python3 scripts/pll_validate_adjusted_player_value.py   # 27/27
```
